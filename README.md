# Health Innovation Challenge:​ Age Related Sensory Changes​
The Winter 2027 challenge is focused on Age Related Sensory Changes, where participants will be investigating and ideating solutions that elderly users can incorporate in their lives to relieve sensory-related stress and challenges. Specific subsections of the challenge include Vision Loss, Hearing Loss, Cognitive Assistance, and Minimizing Impacts of Sensory Experience. Students are encouraged to develop practical and scalable solutions that minimize the negative impact of sensory experiences while supporting comfort, safety, independence, and autonomy.

## Your Mission
You are encouraged to explore one of these focus areas in depth or develop your own innovative solution related to sensory changes for older adults. As long as your ideas are relevant to the overall challenges, you have creative freedom!
