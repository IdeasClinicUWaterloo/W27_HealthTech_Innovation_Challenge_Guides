## Focus Areas
Some sample focus areas you can explore include: 

**Improving Speech Recognition in Noisy Environments for Hearing Aid Users**
- Although modern hearing aids have significantly improved speech perception and listening comfort, understanding speech in noisy environments remains one of the most persistent challenges reported by hearing aid users. For example, settings such as restaurants, classrooms, social gatherings, and public spaces contain background noises which make it difficult to distinguish speech from background noise. Individuals still experience greater difficulty understanding speech in these environments with hearing aids compared to those without.
- There are technologies such as directional microphones, beamforming, and noise reduction algorithms which have improved performance. However, there are still issues reported in real-world listening scenarios.
- There is an opportunity to develop solutions to enhance speech intelligibility, reduce listening efforts, and improve communication in more complex sound environments. 

**Increased Prevalence of Hearing Loss**
- Hearing loss is one of the most prevalent sensory impairments worldwide, affecting more than 1.5 billion people globally and placing a growing burden on healthcare systems. Hearing loss is often associated with aging however, recreational noise exposure to younger populations has emerged as a significant risk factor.
- The use of personal listening devices, headphones, earbuds, and attendance at concerts, clubs, and other loud recreational venuses has increased the risk of noise-induced hearing loss among adolescents and young adults. The World Health Organization estimates that more than one billion young people are at risk of hearing damage due to unsafe listening practices.
- There is an opportunity to develop solutions which promote hearing conservation and safer listening habits among younger generations as noise-induced hearing loss is largely irreversible yet preventable.
